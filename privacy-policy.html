Privacy Policy

At JMA Motorcycle Sales Inc. ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal data in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 – “An Act Protecting Individual Personal Information in Information and Communications Systems in the Government and the Private Sector, Creating for this Purpose a National Privacy Commission, and for Other Purposes.” By using our website or submitting your personal data through our forms, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data from you when you fill out any of our forms or submit documents through our website, including:

a. Personal Identification Information

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Birthdate
  • Age
  • Place of Birth
  • Civil Status
  • Citizenship
  • Contact Number
  • Email Address
  • Home Address

b. Uploaded Identification Documents

  • Valid Government-Issued ID (e.g., Driver’s License, Passport, National ID)
  • Image or scan of the ID

c. Financial and Background Information

  • Occupation
  • Monthly Income (PHP)
  • Years in Job/Business
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  • House Ownership
  • Length of Stay (in years)
  • Number of Dependents (Below and Above 18 years)

2. Purpose of Collection

  • To assess eligibility for motorcycle financing or purchase
  • To verify your identity and submitted information
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • To communicate offers, promotions, and updates related to our services (with your consent)
  • To improve user experience on our website

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process your application or inquiry
  • Contact you for follow-ups or clarifications
  • Conduct credit investigations or background checks (when applicable)
  • Share with partner financial institutions (only with your express consent)

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share your data with:

  • Authorized employees or agents involved in processing your request
  • Third-party service providers who assist in processing, verification, or hosting
  • Government authorities, if required by law or legal process
  • Partner financing institutions, subject to your consent

5. Data Storage and Retention

Your data will be stored securely in our systems for as long as necessary:

  • To fulfill the purposes for which it was collected
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • For documentation and audit purposes
  • We implement appropriate physical, organizational, and technical measures to safeguard your data.

6. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Republic Act No. 10173 – Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of the collection and processing of your data
  • Access your personal data
  • Object to the processing of your data
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase or block your data
  • Withdraw your consent (subject to legal limitations)
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC)

7. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use cookies to improve user experience, gather analytics, and enhance services. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of our website.

8. Data Protection Measures

We use encryption, access control, and other security protocols to protect your personal information. Only authorized personnel have access to sensitive data. We regularly audit our systems to ensure compliance and data integrity.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the law or in our business practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
